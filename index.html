Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about membership and association activities.

Who can join NIEEESA? ▼
All registered undergraduate students of the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at the University of Maiduguri are automatic members.
How can I register for technical workshops? ▼
Workshop registrations are announced via our official telegram and WhatsApp broadcast channels. You can also register through the Student Portal link above.
Are there dues or fees to be a member? ▼
Annual departmental association dues are paid at the beginning of each academic session through the executive financial secretary or designated portal.
